PROOFPOCKET PLUS

Personal document vault

Encrypt files in this browser before saving them to your private Plus storage.

Plus access required

Sign in to an account with an active Plus subscription to open the Vault.

Important: your passphrase is your recovery key.

Choose a unique passphrase of at least 12 characters. It is never sent to the server or stored by ProofPocket. If you lose it, your files cannot be recovered. Keep a secure backup. Do not use your account password.

Maximum 7 MB per file. Supported: PDF, JPEG, PNG and WebP. Files are encrypted before upload.
