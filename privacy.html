PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: ProofPocket stores purchase details in your browser when you are signed out. If you choose to sign in, the app can sync those records with its Supabase cloud database. Receipt attachment controls are now part of the feature branch, but uploads remain unavailable until the required database migration is applied. Browser notification preferences are available in the feature branch; notifications require permission and the app to remain open.

1. Information you may enter

When you use ProofPocket, you may enter item names, store or seller names, categories, purchase dates, amounts, return-by dates, warranty dates and optional notes. Please do not enter passwords, payment-card numbers, identity-document details or other highly sensitive information.

2. Where information is stored

  • Signed out: records are stored in your browser's local storage on that device. They are not automatically available on another device.
  • Signed in: records can be synced to the ProofPocket Supabase project. The app uses email/password authentication and associates cloud purchase rows with the signed-in account.
  • Language preference: your selected app language is saved in browser storage.
  • Export: you can download a JSON file of records available in the app. The downloaded file is stored wherever your device saves downloads.

Cloud sync requires an internet connection and correct service configuration. Do not assume a record has synced unless the app reports a successful sync. Receipt attachments, once enabled after migration, are stored in private cloud object storage and are not end-to-end encrypted. Access is limited by account policies and short-lived download links. Do not upload identity documents or other highly sensitive files.

3. Service providers and network requests

The app is hosted as a static website using GitHub Pages. It loads the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr. When you use account features, your browser communicates with Supabase for authentication and purchase-record sync. Those providers process technical request data under their own terms and privacy policies.

ProofPocket does not currently advertise, sell purchase records, or intentionally use purchase details for targeted advertising. This statement does not cover data practices of the hosting, content-delivery or cloud service providers.

4. Reminders

The development version supports optional browser notifications for return and warranty dates. You choose the lead times and grant notification permission. These alerts are checked while the app is open; they are not background push notifications and are not guaranteed when the browser or app is closed. Preferences may be stored locally and, after the household migration is applied, synced to your account. No email or SMS reminders are sent.

5. Receipt attachments

The receipt feature is implemented in the development branch but is not active until the household database migration is applied. When enabled, supported PDF and image files up to 5 MB can be attached to a cloud-synced purchase. Files are stored in private Supabase Storage, not encrypted end-to-end by ProofPocket, and can be downloaded using short-lived signed links. Deleting an attachment removes its stored object and metadata. Avoid uploading highly sensitive documents.

6. Sharing

ProofPocket does not provide a feature for sharing your purchase records with other users. Cloud records should be protected by the database's row-level security policies so that each signed-in account can access only its own rows. Security also depends on correct project configuration and should be independently checked before a wider launch.

7. Your choices and deleting information

  • You can edit or delete individual records in the app.
  • You can export records before clearing local browser data.
  • Clearing local records removes the browser copy; it does not necessarily delete a separate cloud copy.
  • Signing out does not delete records already stored in your browser or in the cloud.
  • When signed in, you can use Settings to delete purchase records from that account's cloud storage and its browser account cache. This does not delete the sign-in account itself.
  • To request help with account or cloud-data deletion, use the ProofPocket privacy request page. Do not post private account details or sensitive information in a public issue.

8. Retention and security

Local records remain in browser storage until you delete them or clear the browser's site data. Cloud records may remain in the database until they are deleted through an available product or administrative process. No online service can promise perfect security. Use a device you trust, keep it protected, and avoid storing sensitive information in notes.

9. Children and family use

ProofPocket is a household purchase organizer, not a service designed to collect children's personal information. If you are under the age required to create an online account in your location, ask a parent or guardian to help before creating an account.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features and service providers change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the project through the ProofPocket GitHub request page. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, purchase details or other private data in a public issue.

This page explains the current app behavior in plain language. It is not legal advice or a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific legal review before a public commercial launch.
